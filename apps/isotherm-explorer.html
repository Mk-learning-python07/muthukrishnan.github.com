IAST Analysis
Interpret with judgment. These are model predictions, not measurements. IAST assumes an ideal adsorbed solution and relies entirely on the two pure-component isotherm fits; it cannot capture adsorbate–adsorbate non-ideality, surface heterogeneity effects it wasn't fitted for, or azeotropic behavior, and it degrades when the pure isotherms are extrapolated beyond measured data. Treat every result here as a screening estimate to guide intuition and experiment design — always validate against binary equilibrium or breakthrough data before relying on it for a real separation.
